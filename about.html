Scott’s journey

From keeping his distance to opening the hive.

For years, Scott was the last person you might expect to become a beekeeper. Flying, stinging insects made him uneasy, and getting close to a buzzing hive certainly wasn’t on the bucket list.

Then curiosity took over. Learning about honeybees revealed something completely different: complex colonies, remarkable communication, tireless pollination, and an ecosystem built on cooperation.

With every visit to the hive, fear gave way to understanding. Understanding became respect. And respect became a genuine love for beekeeping.

“That’s why the name fits. I never expected to become a beekeeper — and now I can’t imagine not being one.”
