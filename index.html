The man behind the honey

Fear turned into fascination.

Scott didn’t start out dreaming of becoming a beekeeper. In fact, he was scared of flying, stinging insects. But curiosity has a funny way of changing us.

What began with hesitation grew into respect, fascination, and eventually a deep love for the rhythm of the hive. That transformation is why Scott proudly calls himself “The Unlikely Beekeeper.”
